# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

fuseraft-cli is an open project used in professional and organizational contexts. We are committed to keeping it a welcoming environment for contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

Everyone participating in this project — through issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other channel — is expected to treat others with respect and good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct and specific in technical discussions
- Assume good intent — most misunderstandings are not malicious
- Offer constructive feedback; explain *why*, not just *what*
- Accept feedback gracefully; the goal is a better project, not winning an argument
- Keep discussions focused on the work

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or derogatory comments of any kind
- Dismissing or belittling contributions based on anything other than their technical merits
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other venue where fuseraft-cli is represented.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it by emailing **scstauf@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports and take appropriate action, which may include a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban from the project depending on the severity and pattern of behavior.

## Acknowledgement

This Code of Conduct draws on principles from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) and the [Rust Code of Conduct](https://www.rust-lang.org/policies/code-of-conduct).
